07 / FAQGood to know

Questions
answered.

Do I really pay nothing until I see my site?

Yes. We build a working preview first. If you love it, you pay half to launch and the rest once it's live. If you don't, you walk away and owe nothing.

Do I need to take photos or write anything?

No. Send us whatever you have, or we'll use your Google photos and stop by to take new ones. We write all the text for you.

Who owns the website and accounts?

You do. Everything is set up in your name and you get every login. If you ever leave, it all stays with you.

How does the review stand work?

It's a small printed stand with a QR code and an NFC chip. A customer scans or taps it with their phone, and your Google review form opens. No app needed.

Can I cancel a monthly plan?

Anytime. There are no contracts. Just tell us before your next billing date.
